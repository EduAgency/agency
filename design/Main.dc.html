What a German application actually needs

Seven things, in the right order

Your checklist is generated from the requirement set of the specific university and programme you choose — not a generic list. Here is the shape of it.

01

Documents & transcripts

Passport bio-data page, WAEC/NECO, university transcript covering every completed semester, CV, certified translations.

02

English proof

IELTS 6.0–6.5 overall is the usual target. Policies differ by school — we confirm yours in writing before you book a test.

03

German language

A2 is the admission minimum. B1 before you travel is what actually makes the first year survivable.

04

Application forms

uni-assist account and entries — every prior institution, each with its own transcript. Motivation letter. Submitted early in the window, not at the deadline.

05

Financial

A blocked account covering one year of living costs. This is the step that most often decides whether the visa happens.

06

Visa & relocation

Admission letter, health insurance, a registered address. Appointment booked the day the admission letter lands.

07

Spouse or family, where it applies

Marriage certificate with a certified translation, and your spouse’s own document set for the family reunification application. Most agencies treat this as an afterthought; it is a parallel application with its own timeline.
